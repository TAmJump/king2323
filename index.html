Chapter VI · Edge Rules

The questions
everyone asks.

A ritual without answers cannot be law. Twelve edge cases — answered before they happen, in the open.

— Q · 01 —
What if two candidates tie?
同率の場合、どうする？

Answer · Tie BreakNot first-come. Tie resolved by support rate from the country with the largest applicant pool, then re-hashed against a public seed published live. No human edit. No private dice.

— Q · 02 —
When does my Coin expire?
Coinの期限は？

Answer · PermanentCoin never expires. The platform protects the smallest voice — the person who can only ever afford the first ¥100. Their voice persists.

— Q · 03 —
If I leave, what happens to my Coin?
途中退会したら？

Answer · ForfeitCoin is voice, not balance. On voluntary departure, remaining Coin is extinguished. No refund, no transfer. The voice goes silent.

— Q · 04 —
If a candidate dies after applying?
応募後の死亡は？

Answer · Legacy KingA pre-named family member or estate executor inherits the candidacy. The Mission lives. The throne does not vanish because a body did.

— Q · 05 —
Who pays the tax on the Grant?
国ごとの税金は？

Answer · King's BurdenThe King is responsible for self-reporting in their country of residence. Operator pays revenue tax on its 40%. International transfer fees are deducted from the Grant.

— Q · 06 —
How do you stop fake accounts?
不正投票対策は？

Answer · Device FingerprintSMS verification + cryptographic device fingerprint + behavioral signal. Same household, same office, same VPN — all flagged for re-verification before any vote counts.

— Q · 07 —
What about organized self-promotion?
自演・組織票は？

Answer · Bell Cap + Anomaly DetectionPer-IP cap on votes per Bell. Statistical anomaly detection on cluster patterns, geographic outliers, timing spikes. Flagged Coin is held until human audit.

— Q · 08 —
If the operator dies, what happens to the Fund?
運営が死んだら？

Answer · EscrowThe Grant Fund sits in a legally separated escrow account, governed by a third-party trustee. Operator death, illness, or insolvency cannot touch a single yen of it.

— Q · 09 —
What if I cannot afford even ¥100?
100円も払えない人は？

Answer · Weekly Rescue TicketOne free entry per month, granted to participants with a verified four-week voting history. The ritual is for everyone, not only those who can pay.

— Q · 10 —
What if a King fakes the receipts?
領収書偽造は？

Answer · AI OCR + Human AuditAll receipts are scanned by OCR for tampering, cross-referenced with vendor records, then reviewed by a human auditor. False reporting forfeits the Grant and bans the participant for life.

— Q · 11 —
If the King declines the Grant?
Kingが辞退したら？

Answer · Roll OverThe Grant returns to the Fund. The Pool grows for the following cycle. Nothing leaves the system. Nothing is lost. The Bell rings again.

— Q · 12 —
Why 23:23 and not 00:00?
なぜ23:23？

Answer · The Edge23:23 is the edge between the day that ends and the day that has not begun. Between who you were and who you become. Midnight closes. 23:23 chooses.

Chapter VII · The Manifesto

Not everyone can be King. But everyone can make one.

誰もが王になれるわけじゃない。
でも、誰かを王にすることはできる。

— I —

Equal starting line.

平等な不平等の再設計。

Bill Gates. A student. A homeless person. The first ¥100 is the same. After that, the world decides — not the bank, not the lineage, not the algorithm of advantage.

— II —

The deeper want is to choose.

人は"選ばれたい"より、"選ぶ側"になりたい。

Kings, priests, the woman in the club, the one with the gavel — every old institution understood it. The most addictive feeling is being on the side that decides.

Donation, transformed.

寄付を、綺麗事から欲望に変える。

Charity is dull. Selection is electric. The same yen that bores as a donation becomes power as a vote. I made this person King. That sentence sells better than virtue.

A new ritual, on a new clock.

SNS時代の新しい宗教。23:23、世界がざわつく。

News in the morning. Feed all day. Race on Sunday. Now: the Bell at 23:23, twice a week, every week, forever. Time itself becomes the brand.

¥100 is hope. And jealousy. And power.

100円を、希望にも、嫉妬にも、権力にも変える。

A vending-machine drink. A throne. The same coin. The smallest sum a society treats as money — turned into the largest feeling a person can carry: I changed something.
