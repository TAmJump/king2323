STEP 6

Derive The Three

ハッシュ256bitを3スライスに分け、それぞれを Pool で割った余り。

Read the hash as a 256-bit integer. Split into three independent 64-bit slices (low / mid / high). Modulo each by the Final Pool size. The three remainders are The Three. Done. Reproducible to the bit.

pool = 172
h    = int(hash, 16)  // 256-bit integer

// 3 independent 64-bit slices of the hash
h_low  = h          & (2^64 − 1)
h_mid  = (h >> 64)  & (2^64 − 1)
h_high = (h >> 128) & (2^64 − 1)
