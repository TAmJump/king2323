Operator profit is secured at the moment of purchase. The Fund is separated at the same instant. There is no commingling, no later "rebalancing," no opaque overhead deduction. The accounts are physically separate.

Revenue Account

Holds the 40%. Pays salaries, infrastructure, KYC, audit, legal, taxes on revenue. Standard corporate account, standard taxation.

— § 6 · Grant Pool Cap —

If the Fund cannot pay the tier.

Fundが足りないとき、Grantはどうなる？

If the cycle's Grant Pool is below the King's calculated Tier, the Grant equals the Pool. Never more. The platform does not borrow, advance, or fundraise to cover a shortfall. Transparency over generosity.

Voters see the Pool before they vote. The cap is not a surprise — it is the law, declared in advance.

Global Grants. Local arrival.

海外送金。手数料はGrantに含まれる。

— § 9 · If the King declines —

Roll over. The Bell rings again.

Kingが辞退した場合、Grantは翌週へ。

A King may decline the Grant — for personal, legal, or moral reasons. The Grant returns to the Fund. The Pool grows for the following cycle. Nothing is lost. Nothing leaves the system.

The same logic applies if a King fails Royal Duty (does not distribute the 10 free Coins within 7 days), fails the receipt audit, or is found to have committed fraud during candidacy. The Grant returns. The crown is revoked. The next cycle is funded by the cancellation.

— § 10 · Taxes —

Each side carries its own.

税金。運営は運営、Kingは自国で。

Operator
Pays standard corporate income tax on the Revenue (40%) portion in its home jurisdiction.
Grant Fund
Held in escrow as restricted funds; not taxed as operator income.
King (recipient)
Self-reports the Grant in their country of residence. Treated as income, prize, or grant per local law. Operator provides documentation; King is responsible for filing.
Voter (member)
No tax event — Coin has no realizable cash value, ever.

The Terms of Service state this in plain text in twelve languages. No member, no King, can claim ignorance. The contract is the contract.
