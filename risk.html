For each ¥100 payment, you receive one Founding Bell Entry record. That is all you receive. The Entry has no resale value, cannot be transferred, and cannot be exchanged for cash. Any future Grant — if any is paid in any cycle — is paid only after eligibility review (identity, Mission, AML, legal, fund availability) and is not guaranteed by participation, by selection, or by appearance on any public list.
